# Activity 1 - Identify the Connections Between Spatial Concepts

## Purpose
This activity gives students hands-on practice classifying spatial concepts and recognizing the relationships between concepts.

## Task
In small groups, identify the level of  each concept in the following list. Then, for each concept above the primitive level, identify which lower-level concepts it is built from.

> _location, magnitude, distance, direction, boundary, region, pattern, distribution, network, hierarchy, spatial autocorrelation, scale, density, diffusion, proximity_
